Assignment 3 OpenCart exploration baseline
Design owner: Hanlin Yu
Observation date: 26 October 2026
Scope: F7 Add to Cart, F8 Update Cart Quantity, F9 Remove Cart Item
Site: https://opencart.abstracta.us/

Observed data and behavior
P41 iMac, Product 14, $122.00, displayed availability 479, no required options.
One unit was added without a stock warning. Repeated addition merged into one row
with two units at $244.00. Updating to three units produced $366.00. One Remove
action deleted the whole row, and the empty state persisted after refresh.

P30 Canon EOS 5D, Product 3, current price $98.00, displayed availability 20.
The original $122.00 price is crossed out. Required dropdown Select has placeholder,
Red (value 15) and Blue (value 16), with element ID input-option226.
Leaving Select empty produced Select required! and preserved an existing iMac.
Selecting Red and adding one Canon produced one row with Select: Red, $98.00,
and no stock warning. iMac quantity 1 plus Canon Red quantity 1 totaled $220.00.
Removing iMac preserved Canon Red quantity 1 at $98.00. Test items were removed.

Quantity updates 3 to 1 and 1 to 0 were observed during earlier OpenCart exploration
using iPhone. Zero removed that row and remained empty after refresh. The iMac
zero-removal scenario is a provisional regression expectation, not a formal result.

Excluded candidates
iPhone P40 showed In Stock on the product page but a stock warning in the cart
even for quantity 1. MacBook P43 was Out Of Stock; Galaxy Tab P49 was Pre-Order;
SyncMaster P33 showed 2-3 Days. Apple Cinema P42 had many required options instead
of the old Size-only configuration. These products are not used in this revision.

DOM observations used by the implementation
Product pages have a store h1 and a separate product h1; use #content h1.
Availability is a list item with textual status or a numeric count.
The current price is a visible #content li h2; crossed-out original prices are spans.
Product quantity input is #input-quantity; Add button is #button-cart.
Currency form is #form-currency; USD menu button has name=USD.
Success notification uses .alert-success. Cart quantities have dynamic quantity[key].
Update/Remove labels are in data-original-title after tooltip initialization.
Cart rows and amount checks must be scoped to #content, not the hidden mini-cart.

These are exploratory observations, performed with browser assistance to establish
test data and design. They are not nine independently executed pytest results.
No formal execution screenshots or Pass results are included in this package.
